## About Me

I’m an M.S. in Applied Data Science candidate at the University of Florida and currently work as an ML Intern at Aventusoft, where I focus on biomedical AI.

My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning, LLM systems, and production engineering. I’m especially interested in building systems that move beyond demos: retrieval pipelines, multi-agent workflows, evaluation loops, and ML products that hold up in real environments.

I also write about machine learning and AI on [Medium](https://medium.com/@divyanshagarwal188).

## What I Work On

- LLM applications and multi-agent systems
- Biomedical machine learning and signal processing
- End-to-end ML pipelines, from experimentation to deployment
- Data and retrieval systems that support production AI

### ReasonFlow
A multi-agent reasoning system with supervisor-led planning, reflection, retries, and human approval for complex tasks.

**Tech:** `LangGraph` `LangChain` `Streamlit` `Groq`

### Antibiogram Zone Detector

**Tech:** `PyTorch` `Detectron2` `OpenCV`

### Enterprise SQL Data Warehouse
